## Which exam are you sitting?

**Your appointment date decides it.** Exams taken before **December 7, 2026** use Exam Guide v1.0 (July 2026). Exams on or after December 7 use **v1.1**. Both guides are labelled with a version number on the cover; go by the effective date printed there **[Certain: v1.1 PDF, cover and Section 15]**.

| | v1.0 (to Dec 6) | v1.1 (from Dec 7) |
|---|---|---|
| Items | 53 | **70, of which 60 are scored**. The 10 unscored items are not identified |
| Time | 120 min | 120 min |
| Pace | 2.26 min/item | **1.71 min/item** |
| Cut score | 720 of 100 to 1000 | 720 of 100 to 1000 |
| Fee, validity, retakes | $125 · 12 months · 14/30/90 days, max 4 a year | unchanged |
| Weights | per objective (25 objectives) | **per domain only**; objectives rewritten as 24 skills |
| Score report | pass/fail, scaled score, % per domain (diagnostic) | unchanged |

## How the items change

- **Diagnosis from evidence.** Many v1.1 skills start with "Analyzing a trace", "its error output", or "usage or cost data". Expect a code excerpt, a trace or a usage table, and a question asking for the defect or the dominant driver.
- **Longer stems and options.** The three new published samples run 4 to 6 sentences, and every option is a full sentence with its own justification. At 1.71 min per item, reading speed matters more than on v1.0.
- **Same core logic.** The answer still fixes the **cause**, not the symptom. The distractors are still real mechanisms in the wrong place: raise the limit, switch model tier, retry, add a prompt line, add another tool.
- **Answer every item.** The 10 unscored items look like the rest, and there is no guessing penalty.

## Pacing at 1.71 minutes per item

- Checkpoints: **item 24 by 40 minutes, item 47 by 80 minutes**, leaving about 15 minutes for flagged items.
- First pass: read the question line first, then the stem hunting for the deciding constraint, then the options. Flag anything over about 2.5 minutes.
- The [exam simulator](../exam/) has v1.1 modes: 70 items, 120 minutes, drawn by the v1.1 weights, with pacing charted against the 1.71 line.
